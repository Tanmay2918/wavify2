Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my subscription at any moment?

Yes, you can downgrade or cancel your mock plan at any time with a single click. There are no contracts or cancellation fees.

What audio format does Wavify use for streaming?

Free accounts stream in clean 160kbps MP3 format. Plus and Studio tiers use AAC-LC and uncompressed 24-bit FLAC audio pipelines.

How will this architecture migrate to React.js?

Wavify's codebase is designed with a component-first architecture, decoupled state management, and clear view separation that directly maps to React hooks, components, and props.
